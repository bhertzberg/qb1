Quarter Bentury at The Land

Come celebrate my 25th birthday in the Santa Cruz Mountains! This year, my birthday falls on a 3 day weekend - also happy Juneteenth everyone - and I'm inviting all my friends to go camping at a big deck in the woods. This website is meant to brush aside the mundanity of another Partiful blast and convince invitees near and far to participate.

Formal invite

Quick Info

I'm hoping to gather all my friends in the woods and spend a few days playing games, jamming out, tossing dice, walking creeks, hacking sack, and eating. Not much of a strict itinerary but if you have any suggestions let me know.

Date Thursday, June 18 to Sunday, June 21
Location 300 Diablo Road, La Honda
What to Bring Checklist
Offline Guide Download here
Getting There The deck is in a gated community the end of a narrow dirt with no cell service. Drive slow and watch for cars coming the other way.
Gate Code # 1 0 3 4
Connection No cell service. There is wifi and a landline at the house (1/4 mile from the deck).
Sleeping Setup There are plenty of spots to set up a tent, or you can just rock a sleeping bag on the deck.
Water There is running water at the deck for cooking and cleaning. It must be boiled or filtered before consuming - bring plenty of clean drinking water from home.
Flying In? Message me for help getting rides and camping equipment.
Any Questions? Text or Call: 408-394-8462

Fire safety
