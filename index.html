Quarter Bentury at The Land

Come celebrate my 25th birthday in the Santa Cruz Mountains! This year, my birthday falls on a 3 day weekend - also happy Juneteenth everyone - and I'm inviting all my friends to go camping at a big deck in the woods. This website is meant to brush aside the mundanity of another Partiful blast and invoke a feeling of awe in all invitees such that they can't help but be compelled to participate.

Quick Info

I'm hoping to gather all my friends at this incredible spot in the woods and spend a few days playing games, jamming out, tossing dice, walking creeks, and eating good. Not much of a strict itinerary but if you have any suggestions let me know!

What to bring

The Land has a good amount of supplies to support us. We will have speakers, lights, BBQs, pots/pans/dishes, running water (not potable), gas stoves, and rudimentary outhouses. Below is a list of things you will need to pack.

  • A tent, sleeping bag, and pillow
  • Sleeping pad or air mattress
  • Drinking Water
  • Food and drinks
  • A cooler for your food and drinks
  • Ice for your cooler for your food and drinks
  • 3 days of clothes + outdoorsy shoes + flip flops
  • Toiletries
  • Instruments (bonus if you know how to play them)
  • Games to play
  • A book to read
  • Bathing suit and towel (for the creek)
  • Sunscreen and bug spray
