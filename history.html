About The Land

Decades ago, a remarkable guy named Cliff Jenkins got his buddies together on his property in the Santa Cruz Mountains, and they built a deck. The final property on Diablo Road, the deck is nestled in Devils Canyon at the intersection of Peter's Creek and Lambert Creek.

Cliff began inviting all his friends from various dancing communities in the Bay Area to come out for folk dance weekends at his place. These events, hosted every Memorial Day weekend and Labor Day weekend, have bookended my summers my entire life. The crazy people that make up the regular crew became my second family, and Cliff's land a second home.

I can't think of a good way to explain this place so here is a jumble of experiences that came to mind.

Over the years, I've participated in potlucks, danced the macarena, hiked along the creek, fallen into the creek, learned to whip catch on the diabolo, drank a rusty nail, burnt the clutch of an 80s Ford pickup, got stung by 3 wasps on two separate occasions, started playing guitar, sang at campfires, dug holes for pooping, cleared fallen trees with a chainsaw, played Killer Bunnies, briefly unicycled, attempted to slackline, roasted marshmallows, got into poison oak, caught some newts, heard the Ukulele Anthem, shot a gun, celebrated Christmas, ate rhubarb pie, kicked a soccer ball into a bowl of rehydrating mushrooms, tye-dyed my boxers, helped renovate the deck, lost a hacky sack, won a stick race, and washed some dishes.

It's a very cool place and we will have a great time.
