History of The Land

A working draft you can replace with the real family story, dates, and names. The idea is to give guests context before they wind up the dirt road.

Before the deck

The property sits in the Santa Cruz Mountains above La Honda: redwoods, tan oak, and the kind of fog that makes the valley feel like another planet. For decades it’s been a refuge—first as a weekend bolt-hole from the city, later as the place where bigger gatherings started to stick.

Gatherings

The deck went from “we should fix that corner” to the natural center of gravity for birthdays, reunions, and low-key holidays. Running water (non-potable at the site), a long table, and enough flat ground for tents turned it into a workable basecamp without pretending to be a resort.

Today

Quarter Bentury is another chapter in that habit: friends, music, creek walks, and the quiet you only get when cell bars disappear. If you have photos from years past, the historic album on the Photos page is the place to browse—and the weekend album is for what we make this June.
